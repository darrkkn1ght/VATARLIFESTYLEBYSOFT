## 2. Member Guide (How Members Use the Site)

### A. Registration & Approval Flow
1. Go to https://vatar-lifestyle-by-soft.onrender.com/register and fill in:
   - **Username** (3–32 characters, letters/numbers/`.`/`_`/`-`)
   - **Full Name**
   - **LagosLife Username**
   - **Email & Password**
2. Confirm your email via the verification link sent to your inbox.
3. Sign in at https://vatar-lifestyle-by-soft.onrender.com/login.
4. New accounts start as **Pending** and are automatically directed to `/membership-status` until an administrator approves them.
5. Once approved, refresh or sign in again to unlock the full Member Portal at `/dashboard`.

### B. Announcements & Notifications (`/dashboard/announcements`)
- The sidebar displays a live **unread count badge** whenever new announcements are published.
- **Pinned** announcements appear prominently at the top.
- Use category filters (**General**, **Competitions**, **Marketplace**, **Important**) to sort notices.
- Clicking/opening an announcement automatically marks it as **Read** and updates your unread badge.

### C. Competitions, Leaderboard & Achievements
- Visit `/dashboard/competitions` to see open competitions and click into any competition (`/dashboard/competitions/:id`) to read rules and register.
- Once admins publish results, standings appear on `/dashboard/leaderboard` and any podium/award placements automatically populate `/dashboard/achievements`.

### D. Marketplace, Official Money Packs & Manual-Fulfilment Orders

#### Official VATAR Money Packs (By Soft) — Top of `/dashboard/marketplace`
Members can buy LagosLife in-game currency directly from the owner in 2 taps:

1. Tap a pack (plus optional `1× / 2× / 3× / 5×` quantity multiplier). Your **LagosLife Username** auto-fills from your profile.
2. Click **Order** to generate an Order Reference (e.g., `VTR-4821`) and view the official **Bank Name, Account Name, and Account Number** (with a 1-tap **Copy** button).
3. After paying, tap **"I've Paid — Send Receipt on WhatsApp"**. This marks your order as **Receipt Sent** and opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing your Order Ref, Pack, Amount, and LagosLife Username so you just attach your receipt and hit send.
4. Track live status (`Awaiting Payment` → `Receipt Sent` → `Sending Funds` → `Completed`) right below the pack selector.

#### Community Member-to-Member Listings
- **Buying (`/dashboard/marketplace`):** Browse approved listings, select quantity, and place an order. Inventory is reserved automatically when your order is placed.
- **Selling (`/dashboard/marketplace`):** Click to create a new listing. All new listings start in **Pending Moderation** and become visible only after an admin approves them.
  > [!NOTE]
  > Editing the title, description, price, or details of an already-approved listing automatically returns it to moderation for re-approval to protect buyers.
- **Managing Orders (`/dashboard/marketplace/orders`):**
  - All community trades use **manual fulfilment** (sellers deliver directly in-game / off-platform and update status on the order).
  - Buyers and sellers can track every status transition, cancel eligible orders, confirm completion, or open a **Dispute** if something goes wrong (which locks the order for Admin review).

### E. Using the Treasury Hub (`/dashboard/treasury`)
The **Treasury** page connects to the LagosLife transfer engine (`lagoslife-cloud-hub`) so approved members can run automated single, bulk, or scheduled LagosLife Naira transfers from one interface.

#### 1. Connecting Your LagosLife Account
Choose either tab on `/dashboard/treasury`:
- **Account Login:** Enter your LagosLife **Username** and **Password**, then click Sign In.
  - If your account has **2FA enabled**, a verification modal will pop up. Enter the code sent to your email/SMS (you can also click **Resend Code** if needed).
- **Cookie Import:** Paste your LagosLife session cookie directly if you prefer not to enter a password.
- Once connected, your active account name, handle, and live **Naira balance** appear at the top. Click **Refresh** anytime to sync your latest balance and job status, or **Disconnect / Logout** when done.

#### 2. Sending Transfers
Switch between **Single Transfer** and **Batch / Bulk Transfer**:
- **Amount Shorthand Supported:** Type exact numbers or shorthand like `10B`, `50B`, `100B`, `500B`, `1T`, `5T`, or click the quick-amount chips.
- **Single Transfer:** Enter one recipient username, the amount, optional **Transfer PIN** (if required by your account), and an optional **Schedule Time** (leave blank to start immediately).
- **Batch / Bulk Transfer:** Paste multiple recipient usernames (one per line or comma-separated), set the **Amount per recipient**, optional **Transfer PIN**, and optional **Schedule Time**.

#### 3. Monitoring & Controlling Transfer Jobs
- Active, scheduled, and completed jobs appear in the **Jobs** panel with progress, remaining amount, transaction count, and live logs.
- **Inline PIN / 2FA Prompt:** If a running transfer pauses and requests a PIN or 2FA code, enter the code directly inside that job card and submit to resume.
- **Scheduled Jobs:** Click **Start Now** (play icon) to run a scheduled job immediately, or **Stop** (square icon) to cancel a running/scheduled job.
- **Support / Tip:** A voluntary **Donate / Tip** button is available in the Treasury header if members wish to send an optional tip.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your Treasury session token is stored locally in your browser (`vatar_treasury_client_token`) and is separate from your VATAR login. Always click **Disconnect / Logout** on shared devices.

### F. Password Recovery
If you forget your VATAR password, click **Forgot Password** on `/login`, enter your email, and open the reset link sent to your inbox — it will take you to `/update-password` to set a new password.

## 3. Admin Guide (How Admins Manage VATAR)

### A. Admin Access & Security Rules
- Only accounts with **both** an **approved membership** AND an **`admin`** or **`super_admin`** role can access `/admin`.
- **Instant Revocation:** If an admin's membership is suspended or rejected, their admin privileges are immediately revoked at the database level.
- **Audit Trail:** Every meaningful admin action (membership reviews, competition updates, leaderboard publication, marketplace moderation, announcement lifecycle, and role changes) is automatically logged in `audit_logs` and visible on `/admin`.
- **Self-Review Protection:** Admins cannot approve/review their own membership or self-approve their own marketplace listings.

### B. Reviewing Members (`/admin/members`)
1. Open `/admin/members` and filter by **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Suspended**, or search by name/username.
2. Click the eye icon to open a member's **Dossier Drawer** and verify their LagosLife username and profile details.
3. Click **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Suspend**, add an optional review note in the confirmation modal, and confirm.
4. Suspended or rejected members can be **Reinstated / Approved** later from the same table.

### C. Managing Competitions & Leaderboards (`/admin/competitions` & `/admin/leaderboard`)
1. On `/admin/competitions`:
   - Click **New Competition** to set title, description, category, format, start/end dates, and registration window.
   - Manage status transitions (Draft → Open → Ongoing → Completed) and inspect registered participants.
2. On `/admin/leaderboard`:
   - Select a competition, record or update member scores/ranks, and toggle **Publish** when official standings are ready.
   - Only **published** results appear on the public/member leaderboards and unlock member achievements.

### D. Marketplace, Official Money Packs & Multi-Pipeline Senders (`/admin/marketplace`)
Whenever a member places a Money Pack order, a live **gold notification badge** automatically appears on the **Marketplace** link in the Admin navigation bar.

1. **Money Packs & Senders Tab (Default View):**
   - **Payout Bank Account & WhatsApp Settings (One-Time Setup):** Click to expand at the bottom of the tab and enter the owner's **Bank Name, Account Number, Account Name, WhatsApp Number** (with country code, e.g. `234808...`), and **Telegram URL**, then click **Save Settings**. You can also toggle **Accepting Money Pack Orders** on/off anytime.
   - **Multi-Pipeline Senders Bar:** Click **Connect Account** to sign in (with 2FA support) or import a cookie for **1 or multiple LagosLife sender accounts** (with optional saved transfer PIN).
     - Each connected account shows its live Naira balance and an **On / Off** toggle.
     - Orders automatically split across all enabled online accounts in parallel (e.g. a `1T` order splits across 2 accounts as `500B + 500B` simultaneously for faster delivery).
   - **1-Click Order Fulfilment:** Once you confirm a buyer's bank alert/WhatsApp receipt:
     - Click **`Approve & Send Now`** → immediately dispatches the funds across your active sender pipeline(s) and marks the order **Completed** (logged to `audit_logs`). If any sender job pauses for a PIN or 2FA code, an inline input appears right inside the order card.
     - Click **`Mark Done`** if you already sent the funds manually outside the hub, or **`Reject`** to decline the order.
2. **Listings Tab:** Review incoming member listings and **Approve**, **Reject** (with reason), or **Pause/Archive** listings.
   - *Reminder:* You cannot approve your own listing — another admin must review it.
3. **Orders & Disputes Tabs:** Monitor active community trades. When a buyer or seller opens a **Dispute**, the order is locked from normal edits until an admin reviews the evidence/timeline and finalizes a resolution.

### E. Publishing Announcements (`/admin/announcements`)
1. Open `/admin/announcements` and click **New Announcement**.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Content**, **Category** (`General`, `Competitions`, `Marketplace`, `Important`), **Priority**, and optional **Pinned** toggle or **Scheduled Publication Date**.
3. Workflow states:
   - **Save as Draft:** Visible only to admins; hidden from all members.
   - **Publish:** Immediately visible to approved members (or becomes visible at the scheduled timestamp if future-dated) and increments members' unread badge.
   - **Unpublish:** Moves back to draft and hides from members.
   - **Archive:** Permanently retires the notice from the member feed while preserving admin history.

### F. Promoting a New Admin (Super Admin Only)
Role promotion is handled securely in the **Supabase Dashboard → SQL Editor** so regular admins cannot escalate roles from the UI:
1. Make sure the person has already signed up on `/register`.
2. Run this in **Supabase SQL Editor** (replacing `his-email@example.com`):

```sql
DO $$
DECLARE
  v_target_email TEXT := 'his-email@example.com';
  v_target_user_id UUID;
BEGIN
  SELECT id INTO v_target_user_id
  FROM auth.users
  WHERE lower(email) = lower(trim(v_target_email));

  IF v_target_user_id IS NULL THEN
    RAISE EXCEPTION 'No user found with email: %', v_target_email;
  END IF;

  INSERT INTO public.user_roles (user_id, role, granted_by)
  VALUES (v_target_user_id, 'admin', NULL)
  ON CONFLICT (user_id, role) DO NOTHING;

  IF EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM public.memberships
    WHERE user_id = v_target_user_id AND status <> 'approved'
  ) THEN
    PERFORM public.review_membership(
      p_target_user_id => v_target_user_id,
      p_new_status => 'approved',
      p_review_note => 'Approved and promoted to admin.',
      p_actor_id => NULL
    );
  END IF;
END;
$$;
```

3. Have them refresh the page (or sign out and back in) — they will immediately gain access to `/admin`.
